# 🌸 Bloom & Petals Flower Shop

A flower shop management system that lets customers browse and order flowers, book consultations, join workshops, and manage subscriptions, while giving staff, florists, delivery staff, and managers the tools to run the shop day to day.

This is a software engineering course project, developed in four phases.

---

## 📖 Overview

**Bloom & Petals** is designed to bring the whole flower shop experience into one system:

- **Customers** can browse flowers, customize arrangements, place orders, pay online, and track deliveries.
- **Florists** handle custom arrangement requests, consultations, and workshops.
- **Delivery staff** manage deliveries and update their status.
- **Managers** oversee products, workshops, consultations, staff access, and reports.
- **Automated notifications** keep customers informed about orders, payments, subscriptions, and bookings.

---

## 👥 Actors

| Actor | Role in the System |
|---|---|
| **Customer** | Browses and orders flowers, books consultations and workshops, manages their account and subscriptions, reviews and tracks orders |
| **Staff** | Works with customers on arrangements, workshop registration, and consultation bookings |
| **Florist** | Manages custom arrangement requests, conducts workshops and consultations |
| **Delivery Staff** | Manages deliveries and updates delivery status |
| **Manager** | Manages products, workshops, consultations, staff access, and generates reports |
| **Notification System** | Sends confirmations and reminders (external system) |
| **Payment System** | Processes payments securely (external system) |

## Features

### Customer
- Browse flowers, search and filter, and view flower details
- Customize floral arrangements
- Create and manage an account
- Place orders and make payments
- Request delivery and track it
- Manage subscriptions
- Submit reviews

###  Workshops & Consultations
- Register for workshops
- Book consultations
- Florists conduct workshops and consultations
- Manage custom arrangement requests

###  Delivery
- Manage deliveries
- Update delivery status

###  Notifications
- Workshop registration confirmation
- Consultation confirmation
- Subscription reminder
- Order confirmation
- Payment confirmation

###  Manager
- Manage products
- Manage workshops
- Manage consultations
- Manage staff access
- Generate reports
